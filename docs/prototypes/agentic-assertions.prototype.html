What does a non-deterministic verdict do to a fail-closed CI gate?

The decision (#11): Do we support agentic (LLM-judged) assertions alongside deterministic ones — and if so, how do we keep them CI-safe?
ADR-0001: assertions are authored in plain text, compiled to deterministic structured Checks where possible, and deterministic evaluation is the CI default. The CI gate (ADR-0005) is fail-closed: only a pass Verdict lets the pipeline through — every other terminal state blocks. This prototype makes the danger concrete: a judge that flips its verdict flips a green deploy to a blocked one.
